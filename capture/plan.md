# CABAK reference plan

Lane: reference build.
The user requests their CABAK Shopify store using camilaperezsport.com as a
reference and supplies CABAK identity boards. Keep CABAK's brand, palette, copy
and product imagery.

Stack: existing Shopify Horizon theme, native Liquid/CSS/JavaScript.
Content source: Shopify merchant catalogue and theme editor.

Preliminary source observation: the public homepage exposes navigation by new
arrivals, collections and garment categories. Its shipping promotion belongs to
that merchant and is not a CABAK policy.
Source: https://camilaperezsport.com/

Evidence status: public text inspected; browser screenshots, measured tokens,
breakpoints and interactive states remain pending. No pixel-parity claim.

Planned capture widths: 390, 768 and 1440 pixels; verify discovered breakpoints
before final capture. Inspect robots.txt and sitemap before additional capture.
Acceptance: CABAK brand fidelity, usable commerce and owner review, not visual
duplication of the reference.
